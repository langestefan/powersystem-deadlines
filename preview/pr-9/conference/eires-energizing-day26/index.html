EIRES Energizing Day 2026

Energizing Day of the Eindhoven Institute for Renewable Energy Systems

Energy Storage Renewables

The yearly day of the Eindhoven Institute for Renewable Energy Systems (EIRES) at TU Eindhoven: talks on the energy transition, research pitches and short poster presentations by PhD candidates, and time for networking. Free, and open to anyone interested in energy storage and conversion.
